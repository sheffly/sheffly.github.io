this is a placeholder about page — replace it with whatever you want
people to know: what this blog is for, how to reach you, and so on.

a couple of things worth checking before you post anything real, since
this is meant to be an anonymous account:

- strip exif data from photos before uploading them (they can carry gps
  coordinates and device info)
- use a throwaway git commit email, not your real one — github pages
  publishes your commit history publicly
- reusing an avatar, banner, or writing style from another account can
  link the two

not legal advice, just the basic stuff people forget.
