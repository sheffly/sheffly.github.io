this is the first post on this blog. replace or delete it once you've
got something real to say.

to add a new post, drop a markdown file in `_posts/` named
`YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`, with front matter like this at the top:

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "a title"
---

your post content, in regular markdown.
```

jekyll (and github pages) take care of the rest.
