##15 journal prompts for self compassion

1. Write about the time you failed at something, and the lesson you learned from the experience.

I like to think I learned, but I didn't!!! I keep repeating mistakes...

2. Write about what makes you afraid and anxious, and why you feel this way in these situations.

Everything! Uncertainty of any sort! (Ironic, considering I describe my field of study as "decision-making under uncertainty")

3. List 10 ways you can be kinder and more compassionate to yourself.

Stop criticizing myself out loud? I don't know, I really don't think I need to coddle myself more than I already do. It clearly doesn't help me in the best case, and in the worst case makes me insufferable to others and stagnant.

4. Write about all the different ways you are unique, inspiring, and fascinating in your life and the lives of those around you.

I'm already the person "I think you guys think about yourselves too much" is about. Why the fuck am I doing this.

How do I write about something other than myself? How do I stop thinking about myself? What is wrong with me? Other people are not like this.

I really have to stare at myself every time I pass a reflective surface. My old best friend from high school (well, that's certainly something to write about) said I was the vainest person he knew... I think he's totally right.

My goal for the next four blog posts is to not talk about myself. I will instead discuss my opinions on various matters. I think I'll start with talking about men and kinks. I have a lot of opinions on this, I think.

Look forward to that, I guess. Maybe I will try to avoid using the pronoun "I" there. One can only hope.
